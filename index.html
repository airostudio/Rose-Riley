Areas of Practice

How I Can Help

I work with adults experiencing a range of psychological difficulties. You don't need to be in crisis or have a formal diagnosis to seek support — many people come simply because something isn't feeling right.

Anxiety & Worry

Persistent worry, generalised anxiety, health anxiety, social anxiety, and panic. Developing skills to manage anxious thinking and tolerate uncertainty.

Stress & Burnout

Overwhelm, chronic stress, exhaustion, and emotional depletion — particularly in high-demand professional, clinical, or caregiving roles.

Life Transitions

Navigating significant changes including relationship changes, career shifts, parenthood, relocation, retirement, or mid-life transitions.

Grief & Loss

Processing loss in its many forms — bereavement, relationship endings, health changes, and other significant losses that can be hard to carry alone.

Adjustment Difficulties

Difficulty adjusting to major life changes, health diagnoses, or circumstances that have disrupted your sense of normality or wellbeing.

Self-Esteem, Identity & Perfectionism

Strengthening your relationship with yourself — including self-critical thinking patterns, perfectionism, and questions of identity and personal values.

Support for Health & Other Professionals

Tailored support for those in high-responsibility roles — including clinicians, educators, and professionals navigating the particular demands of their work.

Telehealth

How Telehealth Works

Online psychology sessions are conducted via a secure, encrypted video platform — from the comfort and privacy of your own environment, wherever you are in Australia.

01

The Platform

Sessions are conducted via [platform — e.g., Coviu / Zoom for Healthcare], a secure, encrypted video platform designed for health consultations. You will receive a session link prior to your appointment — no downloads are required.

02

Privacy & Security

All sessions are end-to-end encrypted and conducted on a platform compliant with Australian privacy legislation. Please ensure you are in a private space where you feel comfortable to speak freely.

03

What You Need

A device with a camera and microphone (computer, tablet, or smartphone), a stable internet connection, and a private, quiet space. Headphones can help with both audio quality and privacy.

04

Location Requirements

Clients must be physically located within Australia at the time of each appointment. This is a regulatory requirement for the provision of psychology services via telehealth.

05

Emergency & Crisis Limitations

Telehealth has limitations in genuine emergencies. This practice is not a crisis service. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, please call 000, contact Lifeline on 13 11 14, or attend your nearest emergency department.

06

Before Your First Session

You will receive a confirmation and session link by email. Please test your camera and audio beforehand and aim to join a few minutes early to allow yourself time to settle before we begin.
