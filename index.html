About

Rose Reilly

Registered Psychologist  ·  Organisational Consultant  ·  Provider No. 257129EX

Rose Reilly has been a Registered Psychologist and Organisational Consultant for more than 30 years, providing psychological treatment, assessment, program development and consultation across clinical, organisational, insurance and medico-legal settings. Rose works with adults experiencing a broad range of mental health concerns and life challenges and is recognised for her warm, practical and evidence-based approach to psychological care.

Her extensive experience across treatment, workplace mental health, trauma, injury adjustment and complex psychosocial presentations enables her to provide thoughtful, collaborative and outcome-focused care.

Rose has an empathetic and non-judgmental approach to therapy and offers person-centred and recovery-oriented techniques to clients, drawing on evidence-based treatments including:

Trauma-Informed Practice EMDR Emotional Freedom Techniques (EFT) Biofeedback & HRV Training Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) Mindfulness-Based Interventions Psychoeducation Supportive Psychotherapy

Rose also has many years of experience conducting IME-style psychological assessments and preparing comprehensive medico-legal reports for workers' compensation, motor vehicle accident and criminal injuries compensation matters. Her reports address diagnosis, causation, organisational and contributory factors, functional capacity, fitness for work, treatment needs and prognosis, and are valued for their clarity, balance and practical utility in legal and insurer decision-making.

"Rose has a beautiful calmness, kindness and friendly nature that help clients feel comfortable and safe to overcome any vulnerability they may feel toward therapy."

Areas of Practice

How I Can Help

I work with adults experiencing a range of psychological difficulties. You don't need to be in crisis or have a formal diagnosis to seek support — many people come simply because something isn't feeling right.

Depression

Support for low mood, loss of motivation, hopelessness, and the everyday impacts of depression on work, relationships, and quality of life.

Anxiety Disorders

Generalised anxiety, social anxiety, health anxiety, panic, and phobias — developing practical skills to manage anxious thinking and tolerate uncertainty.

Trauma & PTSD

Trauma-informed treatment for acute and complex trauma, PTSD, and the lasting effects of distressing experiences, using evidence-based approaches including EMDR.

Stress & Emotional Regulation

Building resilience and emotional regulation skills to manage chronic stress, overwhelm, and the physiological impacts of sustained pressure.

Grief & Loss

Processing loss in its many forms — bereavement, relationship endings, health changes, and other significant losses that can be difficult to carry alone.

Relationship & Family Difficulties

Navigating interpersonal conflict, communication difficulties, family stress, and the psychological impact of relationship breakdown or change.

Chronic Health Conditions

Psychological support for individuals managing long-term health conditions, including adjustment, coping, and maintaining wellbeing alongside chronic illness.

Persistent Pain Management

Evidence-based psychological approaches to chronic and persistent pain, supporting functional recovery and quality of life alongside medical management.

Workplace Stress & Burnout

Support for professionals navigating workplace pressure, burnout, injury adjustment, and the complex interplay between work and mental health.

Life Transitions & Adjustment

Navigating significant life changes — career shifts, relationship transitions, parenthood, retirement, relocation, or other major life adjustments.

Referrals, Fees & Rebates

Accessing Support

Referral Pathways

Self-Referral

Self-referrals are welcome. You do not need a GP referral to make an appointment — simply use the enquiry form below or contact Rose directly.

GP Mental Health Treatment Plan (MHTP)

If your GP has prepared a Mental Health Treatment Plan, you may be eligible for a Medicare rebate under the Better Access initiative. Please bring or forward your referral prior to your first appointment.

Insurer & Medico-Legal Referrals

Rose accepts referrals from insurers and lawyers for IME-style psychological assessments and comprehensive medico-legal reports — including workers' compensation, motor vehicle accident, and criminal injuries compensation matters.

Fees & Rebates

Session Fees

Please contact Rose directly to discuss consultation fees, Medicare rebates and payment options. Fees will be explained before your first appointment.

Medicare Rebate — Better Access

Clients with a valid GP Mental Health Treatment Plan may be eligible for a Medicare rebate under the Better Access initiative. Eligible patients can claim Medicare benefits for up to 10 individual mental health treatment sessions per calendar year, reducing the out-of-pocket cost per session.

Private Health Insurance

Private health rebates may apply depending on your insurer and level of cover. Please check with your health fund directly, as rebates vary between funds and policies.

Insurer-Funded Sessions

Sessions funded through workers' compensation, motor vehicle accident, or other insurer schemes are accepted. Please confirm approval and relevant claim details prior to your first appointment.

Cancellation Policy

Please provide at least 48 hours notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Late cancellations and non-attendance may incur a fee. Full details are provided in the Terms & Cancellation Policy.

Telehealth

How Telehealth Works

Online psychology sessions are conducted via a secure, encrypted video platform — from the comfort and privacy of your own environment, wherever you are.

01

The Platform

Sessions are conducted via [platform — e.g., Coviu / Zoom for Healthcare], a secure, encrypted video platform designed for health consultations. You will receive a session link prior to your appointment — no downloads are required.

02

Privacy & Security

All sessions are end-to-end encrypted and conducted on a platform compliant with Australian privacy legislation. Please ensure you are in a private space where you feel comfortable to speak freely.

03

What You Need

A device with a camera and microphone (computer, tablet, or smartphone), a stable internet connection, and a private, quiet space. Headphones can help with both audio quality and privacy.

04

Making an Appointment

To arrange an appointment, please phone or email Rose directly. Rose welcomes a brief conversation with new clients before booking, so you can feel confident the service is the right fit for you.

05

Emergency & Crisis Limitations

Telehealth has limitations in genuine emergencies. This practice is not a crisis service. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, please call 000, contact Lifeline on 13 11 14, or attend your nearest emergency department.

06

Before Your First Session

You will receive a confirmation and session link by email. Please test your camera and audio beforehand and aim to join a few minutes early to allow yourself time to settle before we begin.
